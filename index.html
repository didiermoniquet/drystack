How to play

Move and rotate the falling pieces to fill complete horizontal lines. Full lines clear and score points. Clear more lines at once for a bigger bonus. The game speeds up as you level up. If a new piece can't fit, it's game over.

Phantom ✦: clearing lines charges a phantom. Summon it to send a flashing piece through the stack and fill a buried hole you can no longer reach from the top.

An independent falling-block puzzle game. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any commercial product.

F Phantom ✦

Phantom ✦: every few cleared lines you earn a phantom charge. Summon it (F or the ✦ button) to send a flashing piece through the stack and fill a buried hole you can't reach from the top — clearing that line. Move/rotate to line it up; drop to place it.

On touch devices, use the on-screen buttons. Left, right, and soft drop support press-and-hold.
